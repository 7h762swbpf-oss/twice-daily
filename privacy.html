Privacy Policy

Twice Daily · Last updated 2 November 2026

Twice Daily is designed so your journal stays yours. This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Twice Daily does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

Your journal pages, mood scores, emotion tags and settings (including the name you choose to enter) are stored on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or anyone else. If your device backs up to iCloud or your computer, that backup is handled by Apple under your own account settings.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, a copy of your journal is stored in your own private iCloud database so it can appear on your other devices and survive a change of phone. This uses Apple's CloudKit private database, which only your Apple ID can access. We cannot read, export or see it. With Apple's Advanced Data Protection enabled, it is end-to-end encrypted. Sync is off unless you turn it on, and you can delete the iCloud copy at any time in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification content passes through any server.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Twice Daily does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Common questions

Why can't I open a page? Each page opens at the time set in Settings and stays open for your chosen window. Once you begin, you have your writing time before the page locks.

I'm not getting reminders. Check Settings > Notifications in the app, and that notifications are allowed for Twice Daily in iOS Settings.

Where is my journal stored? Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
